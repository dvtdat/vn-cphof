# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The Vietnamese competitive programming community: current contestants (high-school VOI/HSG students, university ICPC teams), alumni checking their own record, coaches scouting pipelines, and organizers verifying results. They arrive with a name, school, or contest in mind and want the canonical answer fast. Desktop-first; mobile is deferred (SPEC NFR).

## Product Purpose

vn-cphof.com is a community-curated, admin-moderated archive of Vietnamese competitive programming history, from departmental olympiads (HSG tỉnh) through VOI/TST/IOI and OLP/ICPC. A public leaderboard, per-person profiles, and browseable pages by contest, department, and organization. Differentiators vs cphof.org: depth (down to departmental level) and community submission. Success = the community treats it as the permanent record book.

## Brand Personality

Prestigious, archival, trustworthy. A permanent record book: editorial and restrained, the data is the hero. Celebratory but never gamified. Bilingual (Vietnamese default) with full diacritics treated as first-class.

## Anti-references

- Gamified leaderboards (XP bars, confetti, streaks, badges-as-rewards).
- SaaS dashboard clichés: hero-metric cards, gradient accents, glassmorphism.
- 3D, skeuomorphism, heavy drop shadows, busy gradients, stock-photo heroes.
- Esports/neon aesthetics. This is an archive, not an arena.

## Design Principles

1. **The data is the hero.** Real tables, comfortable density, hierarchy through type weight and size, not decoration.
2. **Celebratory but not gamified.** Top-3 emphasis is an accent numeral and a hairline inset, nothing louder.
3. **Flat only.** Crisp hairline dividers, strong grid, generous whitespace.
4. **One accent.** Deep blue (#08558C) carries links, active states, and emphasis alone; medals get their own muted tokens.
5. **Permanence reads as restraint.** Motion is a one-time fade-up; nothing moves twice.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Color is never the only signal (medal tokens carry letters G/S/B, NFR-6).
- `prefers-reduced-motion` disables entrance animation.
- Real `<table>` semantics wherever data is tabular; skip-to-content link; visible focus rings.
- Diacritic-insensitive search; Vietnamese-aware collation.
